You can change the colors and logo of Svija Cloud.

To change the logo, replace svija-cloud.png:

    600x160px
    PNG image

To change the colors, open colors.css in a text editor:

    main: the bright yellow-green color
    dark: the header and title bars
    accent: the information bars for specific settings

To update the colors, just replace the percentages of Red, Green and Blue.
